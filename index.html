Cleaning for real family life

The Clean Home Collection was created by a busy mum of five — four boys and one girl — who knows only too well that keeping a family home clean can feel like a never-ending job.

With five children, there are always fingerprints, spills, muddy footprints, crumbs, toys and the inevitable mess that comes with having a busy family home.

I wanted cleaning products that didn’t just do the job, but made cleaning feel a little easier, a little more enjoyable and a lot more beautiful.

So I created The Clean Home Collection.

A collection of cleaning products inspired by real family life — products that are effective, beautifully presented and made for homes that are actually lived in.

Because I don’t believe a clean home has to mean a perfect home.

It’s about creating a home that feels fresh, comfortable and cared for — even when life is busy and the house has just been turned upside down again.

The Clean Home Collection is made for real homes, real families and real life.

Questions, answered

Can I pause, skip or cancel?

Yes, anytime from your account. Changes made before the 25th apply to next month's box. There are no cancellation fees.

When will my box arrive?

Boxes ship in the first week of each month. Your first box ships within two working days of subscribing.

Are the products safe for children and pets?

Our formulas are plant-based and free from bleach, ammonia and phosphates. As with any cleaner, keep bottles out of reach and let surfaces dry before little ones or pets touch them.

How do refills work?

Your first box includes glass bottles. Later boxes contain refill pouches: pour into your bottle, top up with water where marked, and recycle the pouch.

Can I change my scent?

Of course. Update your scent in your account before the 25th and your next box will follow.
