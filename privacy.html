Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

The Clean Home Collection is run by Sam Carless, a small business based in the UK. If you have any questions about your information, email info@thecleanhomecollection.co.uk.

What we collect

When you register your interest, we collect your email address. When you open or click our emails, our email provider records this so we can see which emails are useful.

Why we use it

We use your email address to send you news about our launch, early access and offers, because you asked us to. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any of our emails or by emailing us.

Who we share it with

Your email address is stored by MailerLite, the service we use to send our emails. MailerLite only uses it to provide that service to us. We never sell your information or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop emailing you

Email us at info@thecleanhomecollection.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

This website

This website does not use cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not use cookies or collect personal information. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit.
